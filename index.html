How Kliko works

  1. Stick the tag on your vehicle

    Register your car or bike, add up to three emergency contacts, and put the Kliko QR on your windshield or bike.

  2. Someone scans it

    Blocked in, lights left on, or an accident — anyone nearby scans the tag with their phone camera.

  3. You get the call, not your number

    They reach you through a masked call or message. In an emergency, your family is alerted with the location.

Made for Indian roads

Your number stays private

No phone number printed on the car. Calls and messages go through Kliko, so strangers never see your details.

SOS in one tap

Anyone at the spot can alert your emergency contacts, even without an account, with one-tap 108 and 112.

Works with any phone camera

Nothing to install for the person scanning. The tag opens a simple page that works on every smartphone.

One tag per vehicle

Cars and bikes alike. Manage every vehicle in your garage and renew each tag once a year.
